**Read the full page:** https://rork-ai.github.io/

Rork is for the person who wants a phone app in a store, not a codebase. You describe the app, iterate in chat, and the platform carries you through building, installing and publishing, with separate tracks for iOS, Android and web plus paid extras for backend and multimodal AI. That bundle is the appeal and also the catch, because the further you go the more of your product lives inside one vendor's account.
